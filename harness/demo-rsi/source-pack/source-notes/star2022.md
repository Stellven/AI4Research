# STaR: Bootstrapping Reasoning With Reasoning

- **id:** star2022
- **authors:** Zelikman, Wu, Mu, Goodman
- **year:** 2022
- **venue:** NeurIPS
- **topics:** self-improving-llms, test-time-recursive-thinking

## Summary
Introduces a bootstrapping loop where a model generates chain-of-thought rationales,
keeps those that lead to correct answers, and fine-tunes on them — a form of
self-improvement from the model's own successful reasoning.

## Key claims
- A model can improve its reasoning by training on rationales it generated itself,
  filtered by whether they produced the correct final answer.
- "Rationalization" — generating a rationale given the correct answer — lets the loop
  learn from problems it initially failed, expanding the usable training set.
- Iterating this self-generation-and-filtering loop yields accuracy gains without new
  human-annotated rationales.

## Limitations
- Relies on access to ground-truth answers to filter rationales; not directly
  applicable where correctness is unverifiable.
- Gains can plateau and risk reinforcing spurious reasoning that happens to reach
  correct answers.

1. Workflow feature list

W-01

Ingestion

Workflow sub-stage · 7 L2
  1. W-01.01Request CaptureReceives the user’s question, instruction, desired outcome, and requested deliverable as an intake candidate without prematurely treating it as a formal requirement.
  2. W-01.02Qualified Channel Signal IntakeAccepts a V-05-qualified Slack or internal-tool clue with its permitted context, sensitivity, relevance, and provenance; raw channel traffic remains outside Workflow.
  3. W-01.03User-Supplied Material ImportImports documents, uploads, pasted content, datasets, and explicit file, repository, or URL references supplied by the user; related-source discovery belongs to Search & Ideation.
  4. W-01.04Intake Context BindingBinds the candidate to the authorized user, session, workspace, project, originating signal, and explicitly selected prior artifacts needed for interpretation.
  5. W-01.05Real-Time Intake Deduplication & CleaningCanonicalizes incoming candidates, filters malformed or noisy content, and merges replays, exact duplicates, and near-duplicates while preserving meaningful revisions and independent corroboration.
  6. W-01.06Intake Provenance RegistrationRecords origin, identity, version, timestamp, access path, acquisition mode, and transformations for every accepted intake input.
  7. W-01.07Intake QualificationChecks accessibility, authorization, readability, and minimum completeness, then emits a qualified intake package or an explicit rejection or quarantine reason.
W-02

Requirement Compilation

Workflow sub-stage · 7 L2
  1. W-02.01Intent InterpretationDetermines the user’s actual problem, desired change, audience, decision, and deliverable without selecting an opportunity or solution.
  2. W-02.02Context ScopingResolves applicable context, affected entities, time horizon, inclusions, exclusions, and the boundary of the decision to support.
  3. W-02.03Ambiguity ResolutionFinds missing information, conflicting instructions, undefined terms, and consequential assumptions and resolves them through focused questions or explicit defaults.
  4. W-02.04Constraint ResolutionIdentifies and reconciles authorization, scope, time, cost, data, safety, policy, tool, environment, and output-format constraints with the user’s intended outcome.
  5. W-02.05Requirement PrioritizationSeparates mandatory outcomes, preferences, tradeable qualities, dependencies, exclusions, and deferred requests.
  6. W-02.06Acceptance DefinitionDefines observable success criteria, proof obligations, decision thresholds, failure conditions, and stopping rules.
  7. W-02.07Requirement Contract ConfirmationAssembles a versioned contract with executable task semantics, presents consequential interpretations, and records user confirmation or authorized assumptions.
W-03

Search & Ideation

Workflow sub-stage · 8 L2
  1. W-03.01Search Strategy FormationTurns the requirement contract into technical themes, source families, queries, time horizons, counter-signals, diversity goals, and coverage targets.
  2. W-03.02Multi-Source Signal DiscoveryUses parallel research agents to search open-source projects, papers, patents, standards, experts, think tanks, industry reports, big-tech dynamics, technical communities, the open web, and authorized internal history.
  3. W-03.03Source QualificationScreens discovered material for intent relevance, authority, recency, independence, incentives, duplication, access limits, and potential bias.
  4. W-03.04Technical Signal ExtractionExtracts claims, data, methods, mechanisms, results, benchmarks, limitations, failures, dependencies, adoption signals, and unresolved questions.
  5. W-03.05Signal OrganizationNormalizes signals, binds provenance, clusters related findings, and maps convergence, divergence, dependencies, and opposing viewpoints.
  6. W-03.06Trend & Gap AnalysisDetects momentum, decay, contradictions, bottlenecks, missing capabilities, abandoned directions, and technical white spaces.
  7. W-03.07Idea GenerationExpands a diverse candidate set from signal clusters, gaps, adjacencies, cross-domain transfers, and contrarian combinations while retaining initial supporting and opposing evidence.
  8. W-03.08Search Coverage ReviewTests source-family, technical, geographic, temporal, viewpoint, and counter-evidence coverage before the candidate space is allowed to narrow.
W-04

Idea Identification & Opportunity Screening

Workflow sub-stage · 7 L2
  1. W-04.01Candidate ConsolidationCombines parallel search outputs, resolves semantic duplicates, and preserves variants whose assumptions, evidence, or opportunity boundaries materially differ.
  2. W-04.02Idea IdentificationConverts meaningful signal combinations and gaps into discrete candidate ideas with a recognizable technical or user value proposition.
  3. W-04.03Idea Card FormationForms the governing Idea Card with the candidate’s opportunity, relevance, linked evidence, assumptions, novelty, uncertainty, risks, and open questions; evidence formation is part of this card.
  4. W-04.04Opportunity DefinitionStates the unmet need, technical bottleneck, missing capability, unserved user, or unexploited combination that makes the idea actionable.
  5. W-04.05Technical Opportunity ScreeningScreens novelty, evidence maturity, reachable data, known mechanisms, technical feasibility, and the existence of a credible verification path.
  6. W-04.06Strategic Opportunity ScreeningScreens user or company value, timing, defensibility, strategic fit, adoption, safety, legal exposure, dependencies, and resource implications.
  7. W-04.07Opportunity Portfolio PrioritizationRanks and selects a bounded, diverse opportunity portfolio using transparent criteria and records why other directions were deferred or rejected.
W-05

Technical Claim & Hypothesis Formation

Workflow sub-stage · 7 L2
  1. W-05.01Research Question FormationTurns each selected opportunity into precise questions about the technical gap, target system or user, expected effect, operating conditions, and decision to support.
  2. W-05.02Claim–Data–Method ModelingStructures the technical claims, measurements, datasets, methods, mechanisms, benchmarks, and missing evidence needed to investigate each question.
  3. W-05.03Hypothesis Pool GenerationProduces competing and complementary falsifiable hypotheses, removes semantic duplicates, and preserves materially different explanations.
  4. W-05.04Hypothesis Mechanism ModelingModels candidate solution mechanisms, assumptions, dependencies, alternative explanations, confounders, and likely failure mechanisms.
  5. W-05.05Falsifiability ScreeningChecks whether reachable data, controls, measurements, comparisons, and implementation paths can support, weaken, or reject each hypothesis.
  6. W-05.06Hypothesis Contract FormationCreates a versioned, measurable contract covering the claim, expected effect, conditions, variables, evidence obligations, uncertainty, and decision thresholds.
  7. W-05.07Verification RoadmappingPrioritizes hypotheses by value, uncertainty reduction, information gain, technical risk, and cost, then designs the smallest credible POC, experiment, simulation, or benchmark path.
W-06

POC Implementation

  2. W-06.02Experiment DesignDefines variables, treatments, controls, ablations, sampling, repetitions, measurements, uncertainty handling, and decision logic.
  3. W-06.03POC Architecture DesignSpecifies the smallest end-to-end architecture, data, dependencies, environments, tools, compute, isolation, and rollback boundaries required to test the hypothesis.
  4. W-06.04POC ConstructionUses Builder to construct and integrate the working code, models, algorithms, prompts, simulations, and technical mechanisms needed for the proof.
  5. W-06.05Automated ExperimentationGenerates reproducible experiment or simulation scripts and invokes controlled runs through Harness Core, including parameter sweeps and hypothesis-driven update loops.
  6. W-06.06Experimental Data AnalysisAnalyzes real run data for quality, errors, effect direction, uncertainty, anomalies, confounders, and failure modes and identifies evidence-backed refinements.
  7. W-06.07POC ConsolidationAssembles the strongest code, models, simulations, analyses, logs, counterexamples, failed configurations, and demos into one bounded POC evidence package.

  7. W-07.07Failure Case CurationCurates counterexamples, difficult cases, instability, invalid runs, resource failures, and conditions where the POC or a baseline breaks.
  8. W-07.08Reproducible Benchmark PackagingPackages protocols, environments, datasets, baselines, scripts, run records, scorecards, limitations, failure sets, and reproduction instructions.

  8. W-08.08Decision Fitness AssessmentChecks that the evidence answers the confirmed user intent, supports the required decision, exposes consequential uncertainty, and yields actions the intended audience can take.
  9. W-08.09Human-AI AdjudicationCombines role-separated model review with accountable technical, domain, strategy, and investment judgment, including questions, rerun requests, dissent, and bounded overrides.
  10. W-08.10Evaluation Verdict FormationIntercepts directions with inadequate proof and otherwise issues a confidence-calibrated continue, invest, conditionally continue, defer, reject, or human-decision verdict while qualifying successful artifacts and their limits.

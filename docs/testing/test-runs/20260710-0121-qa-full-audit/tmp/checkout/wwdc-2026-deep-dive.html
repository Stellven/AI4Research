TL;DR

WWDC 2026's keynote (June 8, Apple Park) was Tim Cook's final as CEO, with John Ternus reported as successor.[S10][S11] The headline was a ground-up "Siri AI" rebuilt on next-generation Apple Foundation Models built in collaboration with Google's Gemini — Apple's most significant AI strategy pivot, moving from go-it-alone to a hybrid build-plus-license model.[S1][S3][S9]

Apple framed it privacy-first via a three-tier routing stack (on-device → Private Cloud Compute → Google Cloud), anonymized at each hop.[S7][S9] Every OS line moved to version 27; macOS 27 is named "Golden Gate."[S2][S4] Developers got Xcode 27 agentic coding (multi-model), Foundation Models gen-3 (image input, free Private Cloud Compute tier, Python SDK, open-source plan), and an iOS 27 Extensions marketplace letting users pick Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Grok.[S1][S4][S20] Press read the keynote as "fixes over features" plus frontier AI by partnership; Siri AI ships as a beta later in 2026, English-only, excluded from the EU and China at launch.[S1][S5]

§1 · Strategic Narrative

Craig Federighi said Apple concentrated 2026's software work on three areas: making the platforms more responsive and user-friendly; expanding trust and safety; and a major Apple Intelligence advance via a new architecture powering a revamped Siri.[S10] Press widely characterized the keynote as prioritizing "fixes over features" — addressing search, file-sharing reliability, and the divisive Liquid Glass design — alongside the headline AI push.[S22]

Apple positioned privacy as the core differentiator. Federighi: "At Apple, we believe privacy in AI is non-negotiable," paired with an unusually pointed competitor critique that some rivals appear to be "pursuing AI for the sake of AI, without clear regard for the people … it's ultimately meant to serve."[S10]

  • 2024: Apple Intelligence introduced as a concept; a "smarter Siri" promised but largely undelivered. [unverified — prior-year framing, background]
  • 2025: The Liquid Glass redesign and iOS 26 dominated; the transformative Siri kept slipping. [unverified — prior-year framing, background]

The keynote doubled as a leadership send-off: it was framed as Tim Cook's final WWDC keynote as CEO, with John Ternus widely cited as successor and a transition reported around September 2026 (no official date confirmed by Apple in retrieved material).[S11] [unverified — transition date]

§2 · Operating Systems — the "27" generation

Apple continued the year-based naming introduced in 2025: the 2026 releases are iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27 ("Golden Gate"), watchOS 27, tvOS 27, and visionOS 27 — not "iOS 28."[S2][S4] Developer betas were seeded WWDC week.[S4]

iPhone — iOS 27

  • Rebuilt Siri AI: open-ended questions, on-screen/personal context, in-app actions, swipe-down UI.[S1][S2]
  • Liquid Glass refinements + a personalization slider; rebuilt search foundation for Spotlight/Mail/Photos.[S5][S21]
  • AI photo "Reframe" tool; parental "Time Allowance"; under-13 child accounts.[S1][S5]
  • Support extends back to iPhone 11; advanced on-device AI reportedly needs iPhone 15 Pro / 16+.[S5] Perf figures (e.g. "30% faster launches") [unverified — press figures]

iPad — iPadOS 27

  • Mirrors iOS 27 (Siri AI, rebuilt search, perf) with iPad-specific tuning; enhanced parental controls.[S2][S4]
  • Reported to drop a wave of older iPads — exact models not enumerated in retrieved sources. [unverified — models]
  • Siri AI reported initially unavailable in the EU on iPad.[S2]

Mac — macOS 27 "Golden Gate"

  • Siri AI integrated into Spotlight (conversations + visual-intelligence tasks; screenshot automation).[S4]
  • Design: uniform toolbars, edge-to-edge sidebars, refreshed icons; faster AirDrop/file browsing.[S4]
  • Notably, Siri AI reported available in the EU on macOS, unlike iOS/iPadOS.[S4] Exact dropped Macs not confirmed. [unverified — models]

Vision Pro — visionOS 27

  • Siri AI with visual intelligence; panoramic photos → spatial scenes; Maps Flyover, curved windows.[S18][S19]
  • Reported available in the EU.[S2]

Apple Watch — watchOS 27

Apple TV — tvOS 27

§3 · Developer Platform

Xcode 27

  • Agentic coding integrated across the workflow (prototype, implement, refine), able to drive the simulator, run tests, localize apps, and fix crashes — powered by Anthropic, Google (Gemini), and OpenAI models; Gemini is the 2026 addition.[S12][S13]
  • On-device autocomplete on the Neural Engine — source code never leaves the Mac for a completion.[S12] (Primary TechTimes page returned HTTP 403; corroborated via search summary.) [unverified — primary not fetched]
  • Seven Apple-authored agent skills (e.g. swiftui-specialist, test-modernizer, device-interaction), exportable via xcrun agent skills export; editable Markdown agent plans; new Device Hub.[S12][S13]
  • Reported ~30% smaller, Apple-silicon-only, with iCloud settings sync — single-source. [unverified — single source]

Swift 6.3 / 6.4 (no "Swift 7")

  • Safe Continuation<Success,Failure> making double-resume a compile-time error (SE-0528); Ref<T> / MutableRef<T> for safe first-class references (SE-0519); Optional borrow/mutate (SE-0532, under review).[S12][S14]
  • weak let, explicit ~Sendable opt-out, async in defer, multi-access-level memberwise initializers, and an anyAppleOS availability shorthand.[S12][S14]
  • A reported official Swift Android SDK and "Swift Build" in SPM appear in community summaries but were not confirmed by the fetched Swift.org post. [unverified — conflicting sources]

SwiftUI

  • Universal reorderable containers and swipe actions (no longer List-only; reorder reaches watchOS); lazy @State for @Observable classes (back-ported to iOS 17+).[S12]
  • New WritableDocument / ReadableDocument protocols; item-binding alerts/dialogs; AsyncImage HTTP-cache support; toolbar placement/visibility APIs.[S12][S13]
  • SwiftUI apps auto-adopt updated Liquid Glass on the 2027 OS releases, responding to a system-level slider with no code changes.[S12]

Frameworks

  • Foundation Models reaches third generation: multimodal image input, Dynamic Profiles for multi-agent workflows, a Python SDK, Linux support, a free Private Cloud Compute tier (apps under ~2M first-time downloads), and a plan to open-source the framework later in summer 2026.[S15][S16]
  • App Intents becomes the mandatory Siri integration surface; SiriKit received a formal deprecation notice.[S12] An "App Intents 2.0" branding is single-source. [unverified — single source]

§4 · Apple Intelligence & On-Device AI

The strategic centerpiece: the next generation of Apple Foundation Models is, in Apple's own language, "custom-built in collaboration with Google and its Gemini models," running on-device and on servers via Private Cloud Compute.[S3][S8] Apple reportedly evaluated Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic before choosing Gemini, and drew a firm line between using Gemini's models versus Google's consumer Gemini app — no user data shared with Google, none retained after processing.[S1][S9]

Figure 1 — Reported three-tier Siri AI / Apple Intelligence routing.[S7][S9]

Models & numbers

  • A cloud tier reported as "AFM Cloud Pro" targets the most demanding tasks at roughly Gemini-frontier quality.[S3][S8] Naming should be Apple-confirmed. [unverified — naming]
  • Press reports a custom Gemini cloud model of ~1.2 trillion parameters, a ~$1B/year licensing deal, and Nvidia (Blackwell B200) GPUs in Google's cloud — none Apple-confirmed.[S9] [unverified — press figures]

Developer surface

  • The Foundation Models framework exposes a native Swift API; reporting says any provider conforming to a LanguageModel protocol — including Claude and Gemini — can be routed to via one API.[S15] A fetched developer read instead frames Gemini as a training-time teacher signal, not a swappable runtime backend — the direction is genuinely disputed.[S16] [unverified — conflicting sources]
  • iOS 27 Extensions: users set Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Grok as their preferred provider across Siri / Writing Tools / Image Playground, with per-task routing — ending Apple's prior single-provider (ChatGPT) arrangement.[S20]

Privacy & launch constraints

  • Stated protections: stateless query processing, no Google retention, no training on Apple-user data, and ongoing third-party verification.[S7]
  • Siri AI launches as a beta later in 2026, English-only, not in the EU (DMA) and absent in China; some server features carry daily limits gated behind iCloud+.[S1][S9]

§6 · Ecosystem & Business Implications

  • iOS as a competitive AI-platform layer. The Extensions marketplace turns the OS into a venue where Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Grok compete as default providers — a sharp shift from Apple's exclusive ChatGPT arrangement.[S20]
  • Hybrid AI strategy. Leaning on a custom Gemini model for top-tier reasoning marks a pivot from fully in-house frontier models toward build-plus-license — a notable concession for a company that markets vertical integration.[S1][S9]
  • EU / DMA context. From January 2026 Apple reportedly moved to a single EU model, replacing the per-install Core Technology Fee with a ~5% Core Technology Commission — this is pre-WWDC policy context, not a WWDC-2026 announcement, and the current posture should be re-verified.[S4] [unverified — policy context]
  • Forced design migration. Apps recompiled with Xcode 27 auto-adopt Liquid Glass; the opt-out is reportedly removed.[S4]
  • Leadership transition. A Cook → Ternus handover (reported around September 2026) adds a strategic-continuity question on top of the AI pivot.[S11]
